## 🏆 Keeps (Strong Contenders)

### 1. OV7A4313.jpg
* **Categories:** Nature, Landscape, Autumn
* **Justification:** A very strong, balanced image. The vibrant autumn colors reflect beautifully in the calm water, and the subtle mist adds atmosphere.
* **Suggested Edits:** 
    * Add a slight contrast curve to make the autumn colors pop against the cool fog.
    * Ensure the horizon/waterline is perfectly level.

### 2. OV7A4330.jpg
* **Categories:** Nature, Landscape, Mood
* **Justification:** A great moody shot. The fog rolling over the distant hills and the deep greens contrasting with the hints of fall color make this visually interesting. The log in the foreground anchors the shot.
* **Suggested Edits:** 
    * Lift the shadows just slightly on the trees to the far left to retain some detail in the darkest areas.
    * A subtle cooling white balance adjustment could enhance the foggy mood.

### 3. OV7A4343-2.jpg
* **Categories:** Nature, Landscape
* **Justification:** Excellent composition. The large, intricate stump anchors the bottom right perfectly, and the hanging leaves in the upper right frame the image beautifully, leading the eye into the foggy distance.
* **Suggested Edits:** 
    * Use a targeted dehaze brush over the stump to bring out its woody texture.
    * Add a subtle edge vignette to keep the viewer's eye from wandering out of the frame.

### 4. OV7A4401.jpg (Conditional Keep)
* **Categories:** Fine Art, Minimalist, Open (Not suitable for traditional Nature due to lack of distinct subject)
* **Justification:** It has a serene, almost painterly quality with a nearly perfect mirror reflection, but out of the box, it lacks an anchor.
* **Suggested Edits:** 
    * **Major Crop:** Crop this into a tight panorama, completely removing the messy rocky shoreline at the bottom left. Focus entirely on the abstract reflection and the fog.
    * Boost the contrast slightly to define the silhouette of the distant tree line.

### 5. OV7A4478-Edit.jpg
* **Categories:** Monochrome, Architecture, Americana/Rural Life, Open
* **Justification:** While not a "Nature" photo (due to the buildings and domesticated dog), the black and white conversion beautifully highlights the textures of the old wood, and the dog adds a great storytelling element.
* **Suggested Edits:** 
    * Use the burn tool (darken) on the bright white shed in the center—it is currently very distracting.
    * Burn (darken) the modern-looking house visible through the trees on the far left.
    * Apply a slight vignette to draw focus to the front door of the cabin and the dog.
